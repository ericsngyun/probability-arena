# KALSHI-TAPE-MANIFEST-001 — amendment: exclude venue test instruments

**Recorded as a NEW preregistered rule, not a revision of the old one.**
Approved by Eric 2026-08-15, after `KALSHI-TAPE-MANIFEST-001` returned REFUSED.

---

## The amendment

**`KXMAXSHARDINGTEST` and `KXTESTMATCH` are excluded from the qualification
universe.**

## The justification — semantic, not empirical

> Explicit venue test instruments are excluded because they are **not intended
> to represent organic market activity** and are therefore unsuitable for
> characterizing the qualification universe.

This is a statement about **what the instruments are**, not about what they did
to the result. That distinction is the whole point of recording it this way.

## The anti-cherry-picking fact, recorded deliberately

Excluding them reduces the eligible set **582 → 388** and **does NOT rescue the
failed medium/low separation.** The refusal stands either way.

That is recorded because it is the evidence that this amendment **corrects
instrument semantics rather than changes an outcome.** An amendment that flipped
a verdict would need far more scrutiny; this one cannot, and the sizing was run
precisely to establish that before the rule was adopted.

## Why it was not applied silently

The manifest agent noticed the test instruments **after** seeing which twelve
markets its rule selected — two of them had reached the chosen twelve. It
correctly declined to amend a preregistered rule once its effect was visible,
and escalated instead.

Amending a rule after you can see what it picked is exactly the failure that
killed the EDGE-SELECTION lane, where the "negative control" turned out to be
the in-sample worst cohort and its out-of-sample reversal was regression to the
mean. The discipline is: **a rule may be replaced, but only forward, and only
with its effect on the prior result stated.**

## Scope

Applies to `KALSHI-DEMO-TRAFFIC-CAPACITY-001` and to any re-run of the
qualification manifest. Recorded in the manifest artifact's `selection_rule`
when it is next produced.
